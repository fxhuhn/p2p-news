The Company's balance sheet demonstrates significant growth in both its asset base and real estate portfolio during the most recent reporting period.

As of 31 August 2026, Asterra Estate's total assets were EUR 16.33 million, compared with EUR 8.17 million at the end of 2025, so total assets doubled in eight months.

The increase of EUR 8.16 million came from two sources:

EUR 3.93 million from new investment in the business, financed by platform lenders and the bank. This went mainly into land purchases and construction, CLT factory equipment and materials for upcoming houses.

EUR 4.23 million from the increase in market value of the real estate portfolio, as land plots were prepared, connected to utilities, subdivided and built on.

The most substantial part of the assets is made up of the real estate portfolio.

The Company's real estates are recognized in the balance sheet at fair (market) value using the revaluation model. This enables the balance sheet to reflect the current market value of the developed real estate portfolio.

Acquired land plots and subsequently developed. During the project development process, a number of sequential activities are undertaken, each of which contributes to increasing the economic value of the land:

• clearing and preparing the land plots;

• landscaping, terrain formation and site preparation;

• construction of ponds and other landscape features;

• development of electricity infrastructure;

• installation of water supply and sewage networks, etc.

• subdivision of larger land plots into smaller building plots;

• construction of private houses and further realization of the projects.

Each stage of development increases the level of project completion and, accordingly, the significantly increases market value.

Accordingly, value of real estate portfolio has increased significantly, from **EUR 5.68 million** at the end of 2025 to **EUR 13.17 million** as of 31 August 2026.

This represents an increase of approximately **EUR 7.49 million, or 132%**.

An independent certified valuer recently valued one of the serviced building plots in our Asteres village. Cadastral designation of the valued plot: 8044 012 0577. The plot is 2,032 m2, and its market value was set at EUR 64,000, or about EUR 31 per m2. The valuation is based on recent sales of similar building plots in Garkalne and Ādaži, which ranged from about EUR 27 to EUR 36 per m2.

The plot already has electricity, gas, central water supply and sewage connections, and access by a paved, lit road. That shows how development adds value: once land is prepared, connected to utilities and subdivided, it sells as building plots at market prices. [Link to an to the evaluation (In Latvian)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1RGkd5UD9UiCbQQAhU-ZuE0CIedFc8Ypu/view?usp=sharing)

The portfolio includes approximately 67 hectares of land. Part of it has already been prepared and connected to utilities, and the rest is held for future development stages.

As of 31 August 2026, liabilities to platform lenders amounted to **EUR 9.38 million**, while the market and balance-sheet value of land and buildings amounted to **EUR 13.17 million**.

This represents approximately **1.40x coverage**, or 140%. The market and balance-sheet value of real estate portfolio therefore exceeds the Company's obligations to platform lenders by approximately **EUR 3.79 million**.

As of 31 August 2026, the Company's total assets amounted to **EUR 16.33 million**, while total liabilities amounted to approximately **EUR 10.44 million**.

Accordingly, the Company's asset coverage ratio was approximately **1.56x**.
