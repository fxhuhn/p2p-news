# September update: €5.99 million invested and €1 million reached in Debitum Flow

September brought **€5.99 million in new investments**, while **€776 850 in interest was paid to investors** during the month.

But September was also marked by something new for Debitum – the launch of [**Debitum Flow**](https://debitum.investments/blog/introducing-debitum-flow/).

## September was the month of Flow

For some time, one request from investors kept coming up: **more flexibility**. In September, we launched [Debitum Flow](https://debitum.investments/blog/introducing-debitum-flow/) – a new way to invest for the long term while having the option to request a withdrawal before maturity.

Flow offers **8% p.a. with daily interest payments and no exit fees**. Investors can request a withdrawal when they need more flexibility, while interest continues to accrue until the investment is redeemed.

The response during the first weeks showed there was real demand for this type of investment. In less than three weeks after launch, **[€1 million was invested in Debitum Flow](https://debitum.investments/blog/debitum-flow-reaches-e1-million-invested-in-its-first-three-weeks/)**. During the same period, withdrawal requests were also successfully processed, giving us the first real usage data for Flow beyond the initial launch. We also collected the most common questions from investors and answered them in our [Debitum Flow FAQ](https://debitum.investments/blog/debitum-flow-faqs-answered/), covering withdrawal limits, liquidity, interest payments and how the process works.

## Debitum in Berlin

September also took Debitum to Berlin. Our CEO **Ingus Salmiņš** joined the Latvian fintech delegation, meeting with the local fintech community and representing Debitum alongside other Latvian financial companies. Germany remains an important market for Debitum. The visit gave us the opportunity to strengthen existing relationships, build new partnerships and continue conversations with both institutional and retail investors. International visibility is not only about reaching more investors. It is also about building stronger connections within the European fintech ecosystem and bringing regulated alternative investments closer to a broader audience.
