Who can register on the platform?

Any resident of the EU, the UK, or Switzerland who is at least 18 years old and has a current account opened in their name with a payment institution, credit institution, or its branch registered in the EU can register on the platform.

To start using Revest, you need to complete the registration process and verify your identity.

Which documents are accepted for identity verification?

The only documents accepted for identity verification are a valid passport or a national ID card with electronic security features.

Other types of documents, such as driver’s licenses, residence permits, foreigner identification cards, EU-issued national identity cards without electronic security features, or any document covered with stickers, are not accepted and will be declined.

What risks are associated with acquiring Claims through Revest?

Acquiring Claims through Revest involves risks, including borrower repayment risk, loan originator risk, liquidity risk, operational risk, legal risk, and currency risk where applicable.

Different loan types may have different risk profiles. Business loans usually have longer terms, so users should consider repayment timing, liquidity, and borrower or loan originator risk before enabling them in portfolio settings.

Users should assess their own financial situation, knowledge, and risk tolerance before acquiring Claims. Diversification across loan types, borrowers, geographies, and loan originators may help reduce concentration risk, but it does not eliminate risk.

Can I cancel or sell my acquired Claims?

No. Current acquired Claims cannot be cancelled or sold unless a re-assignment or secondary transfer functionality is available for the relevant Claim type.

Once funds are allocated to acquire a Claim, the Claim remains active until it is repaid, bought back where applicable, or otherwise resolved according to the applicable terms.

This applies to both short-term consumer loans and business loans. Business loans may have longer terms, so users should consider the selected loan term before enabling them in portfolio settings.
