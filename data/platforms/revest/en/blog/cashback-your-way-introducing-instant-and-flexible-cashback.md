From 5 October 2026, you’ll have more choice over how you receive cashback on Revest. Our new cashback mechanism calculates your cashback when you acquire an eligible claim, with two payout options: **Instant** and **Flexible**.

To mark the launch, we’re running a new cashback campaign from **5 October to 30 November 2026**, offering **up to 6% cashback** on eligible acquisitions.

Receive the full cashback amount calculated for your eligible acquisition in your Revest wallet after the purchase is completed. There’s no need to wait until the campaign ends.

If you sell the claim on the Secondary Market before maturity, the cashback corresponding to the remaining holding period is deducted from your sale proceeds. The longer you hold the claim, the smaller the deduction.

Cashback accrues based on the actual number of days you hold the claim and is paid with the scheduled claim payments:

**Business claims:** with scheduled monthly payments.

**Short-term claims:** at repayment, where there is a single payment at the end.

If you sell on the Secondary Market, any accrued but unpaid cashback is paid as part of the sale settlement. Future cashback stops, and cashback already paid is not deducted.

Open **Create Portfolio** or **Edit Portfolio**, find **Cashback Preference**, and select **Instant** or **Flexible**, then save your settings.

**Instant is the default**, including for existing portfolios. You can change your preference at any time, but changes apply only to future eligible acquisitions made through that portfolio. Existing claims retain the option assigned when they were acquired.

The setting remains available between campaigns, but only applies to acquisitions that qualify for an active cashback campaign.

You can check the cashback details for an eligible claim in its **Payment Schedule**. Flexible cashback is shown alongside scheduled payments; Instant cashback is paid separately to your wallet.

Acquire eligible claims between **5 October and 30 November 2026** to receive cashback at the following rates:

| Original claim term | Annual interest rate | Cashback rate | 
|---|---|---|
| 25 days | 13.50% | 0.25% | 
| 1 month | 14.00% | 0.25% | 
| 3 months | 14.50% | 1.00% | 
| 6 months | 15.00% | 2.00% | 
| 12 months | 15.50% | 4.00% | 
| 24 months | 16.00% | 6.00% | 

*Cashback rates are one-off rates for the full original term, not annual interest rates. Cashback is provided in addition to contractual interest.*

For example, an eligible €1,000 acquisition with a full 24-month term and 6% cashback has a total cashback amount of €60. With Instant, this is paid upfront. With Flexible, it is paid over the holding period with scheduled payments.

Eligible Secondary Market purchases can also receive cashback during the campaign.

The cashback rate is based on the claim’s original term, while the amount is reduced proportionally to reflect the remaining term when you acquire it.

For example, a €1,000 claim with an original 24-month term and a 6% cashback rate, acquired with three months remaining, would qualify for approximately €7.50 cashback:

*€1,000 × 6% × 3 ÷ 24 = €7.50*

Actual calculations use days rather than rounded months.

If you later sell that claim before maturity:

**Instant:** cashback for the period after your sale is deducted from the sale proceeds.

**Flexible:** you receive cashback accrued during your ownership, less any amount already paid.

An estimated Instant cashback adjustment is shown before you confirm the listing. The final deduction is calculated when the sale takes place. Existing Secondary Market eligibility and trading restrictions continue to apply.

Cashback is calculated separately for each eligible acquisition.

The campaign ends on 30 November 2026, but cashback already allocated to eligible acquisitions continues under the conditions recorded at purchase.

Cashback does not continue accruing beyond the claim’s contractual maturity.

If the claim is repaid early, you retain the full cashback amount calculated at acquisition. Any unpaid Flexible cashback is paid at closure; Instant cashback is not deducted.

Other incentives, such as Refer-a-Friend and the Welcome Offer, remain separate and can be combined with this campaign, provided the conditions of each offer are met. Their existing calculation methods and respective 30-day or 90-day payout rules remain unchanged.

**Review your portfolio’s Cashback Preference and choose how you’d like to receive cashback on your next eligible acquisition.**
