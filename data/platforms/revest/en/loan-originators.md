Revest connects verified loan originators seeking financing for short-term consumer loans and selected business loans with clients who want to acquire claim rights to these loans. By providing a seamless, automated platform, Revest allows clients to set their preferred criteria, while the system efficiently allocates funds to purchase loan claim rights from verified originators.

This approach offers access to a diverse and growing selection of loan opportunities, all managed in a secure and transparent way. Currently, the following lending companies provide loan opportunities on the platform:

GMoney is a fully digital consumer lending platform in Kazakhstan operated by MFO Unicredo LLP. The company uses proprietary AI technology and the Gmoney.kz ecosystem to provide short-term unsecured loans through a 100% online process.


Its automated infrastructure supports fast credit decisions, real-time portfolio monitoring and efficient operations. The company also applies enhanced anti-fraud and risk controls and has experience raising funding through KASE bond issues in KZT and USD.

MoneyPlus MFO LLP is a licensed microfinance company in Kazakhstan providing accessible consumer financing through a technology-supported operating platform.


Registered in 2023 and licensed in 2025, the company has entered an active growth phase. MoneyPlus combines centralized risk management, portfolio monitoring and operational controls with technology-enabled processes to support efficient scaling while maintaining profitability.

GMoney Group (GM Group Limited) is a financial holding company that provides funding to its Kazakhstan-based lending companies to support their lending operations, continued development, and portfolio growth.


The Group combines centralized capital management with established lending operations and sizeable income-generating credit portfolios. Portfolio cash flows support the Group’s funding model, while centralized oversight enables disciplined capital allocation and continued business growth.
