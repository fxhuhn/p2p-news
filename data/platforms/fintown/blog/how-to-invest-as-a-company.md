Investing as a company at Fintown is easy and offers the same features as an individual account. Follow our simple step-by-step guide to register your business, verify your account, and start investing within 24 hours.

**How to Invest as a Company?**

At Fintown, we welcome both individual and corporate investors. Investing as a company is a straightforward process and offers the same account view and opportunities as an individual account.

Below, you will find a step-by-step guide on how to register a company account:

**Register as an Individual**

First, create an individual account on Fintown. This step is necessary because we link your personal and business accounts. Please note that you must be a director of the company you want to register.

**Request a Business Account**

Send an email to **info@fintown.eu** stating that you want to open a business account. In your email, please attach your company registration documents or provide a link to an open register where we can verify your company information.

**Account Creation**

Once we receive your request, we will create a company account for you and notify you when it's ready.

**Log in and Verify Your Business Account**

The next time you log in to Fintown, you will have the option to choose between your personal and business account. When logging in as a business, you will need to complete the ID verification process.

Once your business account is verified, you are ready to deposit funds and start investing. The entire process usually takes **24 hours** to complete.
