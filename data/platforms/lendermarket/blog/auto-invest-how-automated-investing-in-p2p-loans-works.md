In an increasingly digital world, smart investors look for ways to put their money to work efficiently, without having to manage it daily. While hand-picking individual peer-to-peer (P2P) loans gives you total oversight, it demands time.

The **Auto Invest** tool from Lendermarket provides a seamless solution. It allows you to automate your investment returns while maintaining complete control over your risk, return, and liquidity preferences.

## **What is Auto Invest?**

Auto Invest is a user-friendly portfolio management tool that automatically allocates your available funds into P2P loans based on your predefined criteria. Once activated, the tool does the heavy lifting: checking new loan listings, selecting suitable investments, and ensuring your capital is deployed efficiently without manual intervention.


[**Activate Auto Invest**](https://app.lendermarket.com/auto-invest) 

## **The Core Benefits of Automation**

Automating your investment strategy provides several distinct advantages that directly impact your portfolio's performance.

### **Eliminate Cash Drag**

"Cash drag" occurs when your money sits uninvested, generating zero returns. Auto Invest solves this by instantly reinvesting borrower repayments and interest into new loans, keeping your capital active and deployed.

### **Effortless Diversification**

A well-diversified portfolio is the cornerstone of risk management. Auto Invest seamlessly spreads your capital across different loan originators, interest rates, and loan terms, reducing the impact of individual loan defaults.

### **Transparent Returns & Buyback Guarantee**

Fixed-interest P2P investments offer highly predictable returns. Currently, Lendermarket features a weighted average interest rate of 13.46%, with opportunities to earn up to 15% APY\*. Furthermore, all loans come with a buyback guarantee from the loan originator, covering your principal and accrued interest if a borrower's payment is significantly delayed. This guarantee is a contractual commitment of the loan originator and is not state-backed; it is subject to the originator's financial capacity to perform.

## **Advanced Auto Invest Features**

To give you even more control, Auto Invest includes specialized features for advanced portfolio management.

### **Managing Multiple Portfolios**

You can run multiple Auto Invest portfolios simultaneously. The system operates on a priority basis: Portfolio 1 receives funding first, and Portfolio 2 only begins investing when Portfolio 1 reaches its limit or doesn’t find loans that match its criteria. By pausing or adjusting sizes, you can expertly direct your capital flow.

### **Auto Invest Exclusive Loans**

Certain short-term loans require rapid funding and are available exclusively via Auto Invest. This accelerates the financing process for originators and provides investors with a smoother, highly efficient investment experience.
