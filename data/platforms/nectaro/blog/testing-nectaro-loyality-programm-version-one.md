Your investing journey should get better the more you grow — and now it does. We're launching the first version of our **Loyalty Programme as a Test**, which automatically places every investor into a loyalty level based on the largest portfolio you've held during the period from registration up to the present moment.

And here's the best part: **you're already in.** Based on the largest portfolio you've held with us, you've already been assigned to your loyalty level — no application needed.

**🌱 This Is Just the Beginning**

What you're seeing today is the **first version of the Loyalty Programme** — our starting point. We're rolling it out step by step, and it will keep growing with new features, rewards, and improvements over time.

Think of this as the foundation. The best is still to come.

**🏆 Four Loyalty Levels**

Your loyalty level is determined by one simple factor: the largest portfolio you've held. Everyone plays by the same rules.

| **Level** | **Portfolio** | 
|---|---|
| 💎 **Platinum** | €25,000 and above | 
| 🥇 **Gold** | €10,000 – €24,999 | 
| 🥈 **Silver** | €1,001 – €9,999 | 
| 🚀 **Bronze** | Up to €1,000 | 

**Taking into account your largest historical portfolio, you've already been placed into your category.**

**✨ Everyone Starts as Silver**

Here's some good news for **new investors**: **everyone begins at Silver level**.

From your very first day, you're already rewarded with better commercial conditions and marketing treatment — a positive start to your Nectaro journey, right from registration.

**🔒 Your Loyalty Level Is Locked for the Campaign**

Here's something important to know: for the entire duration of the **"Summer Storm" Cashback Campaign**, your loyalty level is **locked in** based on your status at the baseline date — **end of day, August 16, 2026**.

What does that mean in practice? During the "Summer Storm" Cashback Campaign, your loyalty level — and the cashback rate that comes with it — stays fixed. Adding funds won't move you to a higher tier for the duration of the campaign.

**👀 See It All in Your Profile**

Everything is visible right in your personal cabinet: **Campaigns section** — shows the offers that apply to your loyalty level.

Full transparency, always at your fingertips.

**🔒 A Note on Fairness**

Your loyalty level is a **commercial and marketing classification only**. It's completely separate from any compliance or account-verification processes — your loyalty tier has no bearing on those. It exists for one reason: to reward you as you grow.

**🌍 Grow, and Be Rewarded**

The Loyalty Programme is built on a simple idea: the more you invest, the more you unlock. And this is only the start — we'll keep building on it. Stay invested, watch your portfolio grow, and let the rewards come to you automatically.

**Log in, check your loyalty level, and start climbing today.**
