Our Summer Splash campaign kicked off the season — and you showed up in force. So we're turning up the intensity: say hello to Summer Storm, back hotter than ever and perfectly timed to help you finish the summer strong before autumn rolls in. **Summer Storm** is a cashback campaign that runs from **August 17 – August 26, 2026.**


**How it works**

On August 16, 2026 (end of day), we recorded your total investment portfolio balance, including investments and pending payments. This serves as your baseline.

Grow your portfolio throughout the campaign period.

At the end of the campaign, we will compare your end-of-day August 26, 2026 portfolio value (including pending payments) against your August 16, 2026 baseline to calculate your reward.

Cashback levels based on portfolio growth by [loyalty level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/)  for **existing investors**:

**Bronze**  → +1.0% cashback

**Silver** → +2.0% cashback

**Gold** → +2.5% cashback

**Platinum** → +3.0% cashback

You will find your [loyalty level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/) in your account under the "Campaigns" section.


Cashback levels based on portfolio growth by [loyalty level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/) for **new investors**:

**Silver** → +2.0% cashback. All new customers will automatically receive the "Silver" [loyalty level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/).

**Loyalty level lock:** Your [loyalty level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/) is fixed for the entire duration of the Campaign based on your status as at the baseline date (end of day, August 16, 2026). Any change in your portfolio balance during the promotional period, including additional investments that would otherwise qualify you for a higher loyalty tier, will not change your loyalty level or applicable cashback rate for this Campaign. [Loyalty-level](https://nectaro.eu/blog/testing-nectaro-loyality-programm-version-one/) upgrades resulting from campaign activity, if any, will take effect only after the Campaign ends.


**Example calculations**

**Scenario 1 — Existing investor (Gold level):**

Baseline portfolio on August 16: €12,000 → Gold level (+2.5%)

Portfolio growth during campaign: €5,000

Cashback: €5,000 × 2.5% = €125.00


**Scenario 2 — Existing investor (Platinum level) with Additional Bonus:**

Baseline portfolio on August 16: €30,000 → Platinum level (+3.0%)

Portfolio growth during campaign: €55,000

Portfolio at end of campaign (August 26): €85,000 → growth of €55,000 

Loyalty-level cashback: €55,000 × 3.0% = €1,650 → capped at €1,000


**Scenario 3 — New investor:**

Registers August 18, completes KYC, and is cleared to invest.

First investment during campaign: €2,000 → Silver level (+2.0%)

Cashback: €2,000 × 2.0% = €40.00
