After a well-earned rest following the long winter and spring months, it is time to get ready for summer. And there is no better way to jump into the summer than with a splash! 

We're back - hotter than ever and perfectly timed to make the most of the long, bright days ahead. Summer Splash is a cashback campaign that provides the opportunity to **earn up to 4% cashback for your portfolio growth from June 8 – June 15, 2026**.

## How it works

On June 7, 2026 (end of day), we recorded your total investment portfolio balance, including investments and pending payments. This serves as your baseline.

Grow your portfolio throughout the campaign period. The more you grow, the higher your cashback reward.

At the end of the campaign, we will compare your end-of-day June 15 portfolio value (including pending payments) against your June 7 baseline to calculate your reward.

Cashback levels based on portfolio growth

- €1,000-€2,499 → 1.0% cashback
- €2,500-€4,999 → 2.0% cashback
- €5,000-€9,999 → 3.0% cashback
- €10,000-€25,000 → 4.0% cashback

**Additional Summer Splash Bonus:** Grow your portfolio by at least €50,000 and earn an additional €500 bonus on top of your cashback. Maximum cashback is capped at €1,000 for the bonus from cashback levels (+€500 the Additional Summer bonus).

### 

Example calculations

#### **Scenario 1:** 

- June 7 Portfolio: €1,200
- June 15 Portfolio: €4,500
- Growth: €4,500 - €1,200 = €3,300
- Cashback: €3,300 × 2% = €66.00

#### Scenario 2:

- June 7 Portfolio: €10,000
- June 15 Portfolio: €60,150
- Growth: €60,150 - €10,000 = €50,150 \[Qualifies for 4% bonus and the Additional Summer Splash bonus\]
- Cashback: €25,000 × 4% + 500 = €1,500.00

## Terms and Conditions

**Promotional period:** The Campaign runs June 8 – June 15, 2026.

**Cashback crediting:** Cashback rewards will be credited by July 15, 2026.

**Maximum bonus:** The maximum Cashback bonus is capped at €1,000, excluding the Additional Summer Splash bonus of €500, which is granted only to investors whose portfolio grows by at least €50,000.

**Verification required:** Portfolio growth is calculated based on the recorded outstanding portfolio value and pending payments at the beginning of the campaign and compared to the last day of the campaign period. New investors that have not been registered on June 8 will be recorded with €0 portfolio balance and portfolio growth will be determined by the investment balance at the final day of the investment period. Investors must ensure their accounts and investments comply with Nectaro’s terms and policies. Investors must have completed registration and KYC process and be able to invest the funds.

**Other bonuses:** Bonus can be combined with Newbies bonus and other bonuses.
