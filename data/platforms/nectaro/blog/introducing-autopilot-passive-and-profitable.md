Managing investments shouldn’t take up your time. With **AutoPilot**, our new predefined strategy in Auto Invest, you can put your portfolio on autopilot — literally.

When opening updated Auto Invest section in your personal cabinet and chose button Create new - you’ll now see two options:

- **AutoPilot strategy** – a predefined strategy that automatically invests across available instruments to achieve broad diversification.
- **Custom strategy** – customize your own investment strategy based on your preferred return, maturity and risk parameters.

### **🚀 What is AutoPilot?**

AutoPilot is a ready-made investment strategy designed for simplicity and diversification. Instead of setting everything up manually and updating, you can activate a fully automated, rule-based way of investing. .

It offers truly passive hands-off investing, and the potential to achieve same returns to those typically available on the platform.

### **⚙️ How It Works**

AutoPilot automatically invests your funds into eligible instruments across the platform:

Investing from €10 up to €250 per instrument

Running multiple times per day

Automatically allocating available funds

Its core logic is simple:

It prioritizes older available instruments and allocates funds step by step across the portfolio. Note, that most of the loans on Nectaro are getting funding within the one months.

This creates a consistent, diversified investment flow — without manual effort.

Before activating, you choose how much you want to invest. That amount becomes your allocation limit.

Your investments may span different:

- Countries and markets
- Instrument types
- Lending originators

If no opportunities are available, your funds simply wait for the next run.

### **🤖 Fully Automated, Always in Your Control**

Once active, AutoPilot runs on its own — no manual action needed.

But you stay in control:

- Pause anytime
- Resume whenever you want
- Keep full ownership of your funds

### **🌍 A Smarter Way to Invest**

AutoPilot is built for those who want a simple, efficient way to invest — without constant management. It can automatically reinvest returns, including early repayments, so your money keeps working for you instead of sitting idle in your account.

Turn it on. Set your limit. Let it work.

**That’s investing on AutoPilot.**
