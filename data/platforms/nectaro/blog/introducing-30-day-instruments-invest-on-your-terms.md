For many of our investors, the decision to invest isn't just about returns. It's about confidence. Confidence that when life happens - an unexpected opportunity, an unplanned expense, a shift in priorities - your capital isn't locked away beyond reach.

That's why today we're excited to announce the launch of **30-day instruments** on Nectaro: our shortest-term investment option yet, designed to put you firmly in control of your money.

## We Heard You

Over the past months, we've received consistent feedback from our community: "I'd love to invest more, but I'm currently not comfortable tying up funds for 6 or 12 months."

This is completely understandable. Whether you're managing a business with variable cash flow, building an emergency reserve, or simply prefer to keep your options open - long commitment windows can feel like a barrier rather than a benefit.

We took that feedback seriously, and we acted on it.

## Introducing 30-Day Instruments

Starting today, you can deploy your funds into 30-day investment instruments on Nectaro - earning a competitive return over a short, predictable cycle, with your capital back in your hands within a month.

This isn't a compromise. It's a purpose-built solution for investors who value:

- **Liquidity** - know exactly when your funds will be available again;
- **Flexibility** - reinvest, withdraw, or redirect as your needs evolve;
- **Simplicity** - a clear, single-month horizon with no surprises.

## The Best of Both Worlds

Short-term doesn't mean low ambition. 30-day instruments on Nectaro let you put idle cash to work - even funds you've been holding back because longer commitments didn't feel right.

Think of it this way: money sitting in a current account earns nothing. Money you were hesitant to invest because of liquidity concerns earns nothing. Now, it can earn something - without sacrificing the flexibility you need.

For investors already active on Nectaro, 30-day instruments also complement your existing portfolio beautifully. You can maintain your longer-term holdings for higher yield potential while keeping a portion of your capital in the short-term cycle, ready to move when you need it.

## Our Commitment

At Nectaro, we believe great investing should be accessible - not just to those who can afford to lock away capital indefinitely, but to anyone with a smart financial mindset and a real-world life to navigate.

This launch is part of our broader commitment to building a platform that responds to your needs - not the other way around. We will continue listening, iterating, and bringing new solutions to life based on what matters most to our community.
